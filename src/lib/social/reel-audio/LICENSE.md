# Reel soundtracks: licence

These four tracks are original works made for Royal Fitness Club by `scripts/make-reel-music.py`.
Every sound in them (kick, clap, hats, bass, pads, lead) is synthesised from sine, saw and noise
waves in that script. No samples, loops, presets or recordings from anyone else are used, so there
is no third-party licence and nothing for Instagram's or Facebook's audio matching to flag.

Owner: Royal Fitness Club. Use them anywhere the gym publishes.

To add a track, give it a new entry in the script's `TRACKS` list and run it, or drop in any
`.mp3` you hold a written commercial licence for (keep that licence next to it in this folder).
Never add a song downloaded from YouTube, Instagram or a music app: Meta mutes or removes Reels
that use copyrighted music.
