3D anatomy models in this folder (muscles.glb, bones.glb, skin.glb)

Adapted from Z-Anatomy (https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0,
which is derived from BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS),

Changes: converted from FBX to glTF, mesh-simplified and compressed, label objects removed,
muscles grouped by training region, and genital / pelvic-floor regions removed from the skin.
Rebuilt with scripts/build-anatomy.mjs.
